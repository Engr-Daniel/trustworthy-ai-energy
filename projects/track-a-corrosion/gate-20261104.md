## Evaluation freeze

`protocol.json` prespecifies source review budgets and endpoints. Keep tuning and held-out labels separate. Freeze source-calibrated thresholds before evaluating held-out domains. Measure achieved target workload, accepted error and accepted misses with explicit denominators. Do not treat a threshold meeting a source budget as meeting the same budget under shift. Keep all crops/augmentations of a source image in its group; inspect near-duplicates before interpreting independence.

Before a main run, prespecify a group-aware interval/bootstrap plan, minimum usable positive counts and the effect size the available groups can detect. No exchangeability-based guarantee will be claimed under uncontrolled acquisition shift. The current evaluator is a point-estimate harness, not a conformal certificate.

## Meaning of a negative result

If simple confidence/random policies match uncertainty methods, report that additional complexity was unsupported. If shift increases both accepted errors and review workload, that failure is evidence about policy transport. If labels or groups cannot sustain the evaluation, report feasibility failure rather than a successful industrial detector. Do not change endpoints after inspecting held-out outcomes.
