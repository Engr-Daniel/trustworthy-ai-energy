# Research-agent working instructions

Read memory.md, task.md and the current protocol before work. The user's latest instructions take precedence.

1. Preserve record identity and the dated research story. Append amendments instead of rewriting history.
2. Scope broadly before assigning confirmed clusters or making field-wide gap claims.
3. Use primary papers/publisher records for claims. Bibliographic APIs support metadata, not experimental interpretation.
4. Save exact searches, source/platform, fields, limits, date, raw responses/exports and checksums. Log failed requests and retries.
5. Keep identification events, deduplicated report records and studies as different units. Verify version linkage explicitly.
6. Keep screening, extraction and interpretation separate. Do not claim independent dual review by using two AI passes.
7. Never invent authors, DOI, venue, date, access, dataset properties or citation verification. Use explicit unknown states.
8. Do not discard records through title heuristics. Automated queue flags prioritise reading; human eligibility decisions need reasons.
9. Cite evidence locators for extracted scientific fields. Not extracted is different from Not reported.
10. Keep taxonomy assignments provisional until pilot charting and codebook review support them.
11. Keep CSV/JSON as the replayable source of truth and the workbook/Google Sheet synchronised deliberately. Read fresh shared-sheet changes before overwriting them.
12. Run validate_scope.py after edits to records and counts. Rebuild and verify the workbook before release.
13. Do not begin model experiments before a dated novelty/data/compute/evaluation gate decision.
14. Maintain a single active work plan. Do not spawn subagents unless Daniel or applicable instructions explicitly request parallel agents.

No deployment, repository publication or external communication should be inferred from this file.
