# Title/abstract screening notes - AI-assisted under delegation (protocol v0.4)
Started 22 October 2026. Reviewer: Claude (AI assistant), delegated by Daniel Oyewale. These are operational clarifications applied consistently while screening; they do not change protocol v0.4 eligibility.

1. Accuracy-oriented methods (new architectures, distillation, reconstruction, prompts) without a signalled reliability dimension: Exclude TA05. A generic claim ("robust", "reliable") without a corresponding design or evaluation does not count.
2. Zero-/few-shot category transfer counts as R3 only when the record frames it as domain or distribution shift, cross-domain generalisation or robustness. Otherwise TA05.
3. Reviews: Background/supporting when they address a reliability dimension or broadly review AI visual inspection in a core context. Narrow-technique reviews without reliability: TA05.
4. Title-only records (no captured abstract): exclude only when the title clearly fails a criterion. If the title signals a reliability dimension but context or modality is uncertain, retrieve the full text.
5. Non-visual records: Exclude TA02 unless the adjacent rule applies (a transferable reliability method or evidence for industrial inspection or condition monitoring); then Background/supporting.
6. Same-title duplicates receive the same decision; version linkage is handled separately.
7. Surveillance/video anomaly detection, autonomous driving, medical-only and network-security tasks: TA01/TA03.
8. (Added 22 Oct 2026 after batch 101.) Uncertainty used only as an internal training or selection device (active-learning sampling, uncertainty-weighted pseudo-labels, uncertainty gates inside a network) without evaluating predictive uncertainty, calibration or decision reliability as an output: TA05. Exception: an evaluated human-verification workflow (R5). Earlier decisions were recoded in corrections-01.txt.
9. Detection-head "confidence" or IoU-quality estimation counts as calibration (R1) only when calibration is measured.
10. Adversarial-attack robustness evaluations count under R3 (robustness) and are tagged "adversarial" for charting.
11. Knowledge-based or fuzzy-rule inspection systems handling uncertainty without a learned model fail condition 3. They are kept as Background/supporting for the historical lineage of uncertainty handling in automated inspection (C0049 and O0063 recoded in corrections-01.txt).
12. (Added 22 Oct 2026 at batch 225.) Imaging NDT outside the protocol's listed modalities (GPR radargrams, ultrasonic phased-array/TFM images) is not core eligible. When such a record proposes or evaluates a reliability method that transfers to visual inspection (sim-to-real adaptation, cross-domain generalisation), it is Background/supporting under the adjacent rule (note 5). Signal-to-image encodings of vibration, current or acoustic data remain TA02.
13. (Added 22 Oct 2026 at batch 231.) Records that look like a preprint, thesis or journal version of an already screened record receive a consistent decision, and the probable linkage is stated in the rationale for later version-linkage verification. Linkage is not asserted at screening.
14. (Added 22 Oct 2026 at batch 265.) Non-visual fault-diagnosis records whose contribution is generic domain adaptation, domain generalisation, open-set or OOD detection on signal or process data are TA02: that literature is large and its visual counterparts are already in the corpus. Background/supporting under note 5 is kept for non-visual records whose central contribution is a reliability method that is sparse in visual inspection (conformal or selective prediction, calibration, evidential or Bayesian uncertainty quantification, sensor-failure or missing-sensor handling). Earlier decisions were checked against this rule; O3521 and O3590 were coded TA02 under it.
