# Reporting readiness
Frameworks: PRISMA-ScR and PRISMA-S. This is a readiness crosswalk, not a claim of compliance.
| Area | Current evidence | Remaining work |
|---|---|---|
| Review objective and eligibility | Protocol v0.3 drafted | Pilot and freeze criteria |
| Protocol transparency | Dated draft and amendments | Prospective registration / reviewer agreement |
| Search reporting | Exact API requests, dates, caps, raw snapshots and hashes | Subject-database queries and complete exports |
| Record selection | Occurrence and report ledgers, reasons and reviewer fields | Assign reviewers and perform screening |
| Evidence charting | Forty-column corpus and a pilot matrix | Full-text extraction and human audit |
| Synthesis | Working prompts and unassigned taxonomy | Field map and final conclusions |
| Limits and support | Pilot caps and unknown states explicit | Declare final restrictions, funding and competing interests |
No final manuscript flow diagram should present these pilot intake counts as a completed selection process.
